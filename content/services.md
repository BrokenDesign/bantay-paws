### Dog Rescue & Rehabilitation

We respond to reports of injured, abandoned, or mistreated dogs within Lambunao and nearby barangays. Each rescued dog receives medical evaluation, treatment, and a safe place to recover.</br></br>

### Shelter & Sanctuary Care

Our sanctuary provides clean, secure, and nurturing spaces where dogs can heal physically and emotionally. They receive daily feeding, exercise, and socialization suited to their needs.</br></br>

### Vaccination Programs

To protect both pets and the community, the foundation conducts:

- Anti-rabies vaccination
- Anti-tick and flea preventive treatments
- Deworming for puppies and adult dogs

These programs are offered through scheduled barangay missions and on-site at the sanctuary.</br></br>

### Spay & Neuter Assistance

To help control the stray population, Bantay Paws partners with veterinarians to offer low-cost or sponsored spay/neuter procedures. Our goal is long-term population control through humane means.</br></br>

### Adoption & Rehoming

We match rescued dogs with responsible, loving families. Each adopted dog undergoes medical screening and personality assessment to ensure the best placement.</br></br>

### Community Education & Outreach

We conduct educational campaigns about:

- Responsible pet ownership
- Vaccination importance
- Bite prevention
- Humane treatment of animals

These outreach programs empower households and barangays to take an active role in animal welfare.</br></br>

### Feeding Missions

Regular feeding activities are held in areas with high stray populations, ensuring dogs in the community receive basic nutrition and hydration while longer-term solutions are developed.</br></br>

### Emergency Response Support

For urgent cases—such as road accidents or severely ill strays—we provide immediate help and coordinate with vets and volunteers for rapid intervention.</br></br>

### Volunteer & Youth Programs

We offer opportunities for students, families, and animal lovers to volunteer in:

- Sanctuary cleaning
- Dog walking & enrichment
- Feeding drives
- Vaccination missions

These programs nurture compassion and community involvement.</br></br>
