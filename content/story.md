## How Bantay Paws Foundation Started

Bantay Paws Foundation began with a simple vision: to create a safe space for the forgotten and vulnerable dogs of Lambunao, Iloilo. What started as a personal act of compassion soon grew into a mission that touched an entire community.</br></br>

The idea was born from witnessing the daily struggles of stray dogs—animals roaming the streets for food, battling illness, and surviving without shelter or protection. Seeing their quiet suffering made it clear that something had to be done. There was no local sanctuary, no structured program for vaccination, and no place where these dogs could heal. That gap became the spark.</br></br>

Driven by a deep love for animals and a belief that every dog deserves dignity and care, the foundation began its work: rescuing, feeding, and providing basic medical support using personal resources and community help. What started with just a few dogs quickly grew as more people saw the effort and stepped forward to help.</br></br>

From those humble beginnings came a larger dream—to build a sanctuary that offers safety, rehabilitation, and hope. A place where dogs are not only sheltered but truly cared for. The 600-sqm sanctuary concept was envisioned to give them space to heal, run, and feel loved again.</br></br>

Bantay Paws Foundation continues to grow because of compassion, volunteerism, and the belief that kindness can transform lives—both human and animal. Today, it stands as a testament to what a community can build when it chooses empathy and action.</br></br>
